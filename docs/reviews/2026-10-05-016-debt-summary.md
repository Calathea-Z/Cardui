# Debt summary

Date: October 5, 2026

## Increment

Debts has a summary above the cards. It totals the recorded balances, interest for one month, minimums, and how much of the credit limits are in use. A passed due date, a promotion, and missing inputs sit in short rows under those figures. A debt with two balances explains that choice on its own card. There is no score.

## Changes

- Interest is the recorded balance times the rate in effect, divided by 12, rounded to cents. A promotional APR is that rate through its end date, including that date. After the end date, the regular APR is used. A missing balance or rate stays unknown and is left out of the total. A known zero stays zero. One line under Summary says these totals are for the debts below, and that interest is this month, not the total left to pay. A debt with two balances is not listed in that summary.
- Minimums add up the same way. A missing minimum is counted so the total is not treated as complete.
- Utilization is the share of the known revolving limits in use. A revolving debt needs both a balance and a credit limit. At 30 percent the card says 30 percent or more of the limit is in use. At 90 percent it says 90 percent instead. An installment debt is not part of that ratio.
- A due date before today is called out. The screen says the date has passed and that Cardui does not know whether it was paid. A due date of today is not treated as passed. A blank due date is a missing input.
- A promotion that ends today or within 60 days is named, with the APR that follows when that APR is known. A promotion that has already ended is named too. A promotional rate with no end date, or an end date with no promotional rate, stays missing.
- An APR of 20 percent or higher is named as that threshold. The dollar interest stays the cost.
- A linked credit card or loan is shown beside the recorded balance when the amounts differ. Both amounts and dates sit in one comparison. Recorded is marked in use. The account amount is marked not in use. The plan keeps the debt's dated balance for interest, utilization, and the total. Use this balance copies that snapshot onto the debt after confirmation. The account balance, APR, minimum, and due date stay as they are. A cash account is not offered. A balance with no date, a different currency, a negative amount, or an amount too large to store is shown and cannot be copied.
- Totals are kept per currency. Another currency is not added in.
- An account with no debt does not become a debt. Connecting a card still does not create one.
- The summary is `GET /api/debts/summary`. Choosing the account balance is `POST /api/debts/{id}/use-account-balance`. No migration.

## Data changes

None until someone chooses an account balance. That writes the debt's balance and the date it was true. It does not change the account, and it does not change APR, minimum, or due date.

## Manual verification

Restart the API before these steps so it loads this build. No migration. Income, bills, and account balances should stay the same unless you confirm step 6.

1. Open Debts with a debt that has a balance, an APR, and a minimum.
   Expected: Summary is above the cards, with Recorded, Interest this month, Minimums, and Limits in use. The line under Summary says the totals are for the debts below, and that interest is each recorded balance times its rate, divided by 12. That is this month, not the total left to pay. A missing amount says Unknown. There is no score, and the word Health is not on the page.
2. Edit that debt and clear the APR. Save.
   Expected: Interest this month is unknown. The missing APR is not shown as zero.
3. Set the due date to yesterday.
   Expected: The card and Summary say the due date has passed, and that the payment status is unknown.
4. Open Promotional terms. Enter a promo APR that ends within 60 days, and a higher regular APR. Save.
   Expected: Interest uses the promotional rate. The card says the date it ends and the APR after that.
5. On a revolving debt, set a credit limit so the balance is at least 30 percent of it. Then set another, or the same one, so the balance is at least 90 percent.
   Expected: The card names 30 percent or more, or 90 percent or more. It does not turn that into a grade.
6. Link a credit card or loan whose latest balance differs from the debt.
   Expected: The card, not Summary, says Two balances. It says the totals use the recorded balance, and that you can choose the account balance instead. The two amounts sit side by side with their dates. Recorded is marked in use. Account is marked not in use. The account balance on Accounts is unchanged. Choose Use this balance and confirm.
   Expected: The debt's balance and date become the account's. APR, minimum, and due date stay as they were. The account balance is unchanged. The disagreement goes away.
7. Link a cash account, or a card that has a balance and no dated snapshot.
   Expected: A cash balance is not offered as the debt balance. A balance with no date can be seen and cannot be copied.
8. On a phone-width window, open Debts.
   Expected: The four figures sit in two columns. Use this balance, when it is offered, is easy to tap. There is still no score.

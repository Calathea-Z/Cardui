# Fact basis

## Increment

Phase 4 item 5. Planning inputs are labeled as a user statement, an imported
fact, an estimate, or an unverified suggestion. An inferred recurring expense

started.

## Decision

- `IPlanningFacts.DescribeAsync` returns the labeled inputs. There is no
  route and no screen. `IPlanningModel` is not called. Nothing is written.
- A user statement is a paycheck, a saved bill, a debt term the person set,
  a manual cash balance, a debt balance or credit limit they kept, monthly
  living spending, or a note on the open conversation.
- An imported fact is cash from a connected account or a file, or a debt
  balance or credit limit taken from a connected account. A stale synced
  balance stays imported. It is not rewritten as an estimate or as zero.
- An estimate is the monthly average of a paycheck. It has no date. Irregular
  pay has no average. Low, strong, and gross pay stay on the income record as
  amounts the person entered. This slice does not label them again.
- An unverified suggestion is a recurring pattern noticed in activity, or a
  connected account that looks like a debt. The pattern keeps one typical
  payment. The match carries no amount. An account the debt already follows
  is not suggested again.
- A missing debt term is omitted. That omission is not zero. A known zero
  minimum stays zero. A note stays words, so `unknown` does not become cash
  or a bill. A closed conversation's notes are left out of the list, and the
  note row stays.
- A saved bill is the only confirmed obligation. Saving a bill on the
  existing bill form, including one saved from a suggestion, is that
  confirmation. Describe does not save a bill, hide a pattern, or follow an
  account.
- A credit card, a loan, and an investment are not labeled as cash. A card
  balance does not become a debt. Remaining term, promotional terms, savings
  goals other than living spending, allocation, forecasts, and a tool
  proposal are not labeled here. Transactions and their descriptions are not
  returned.
- Another household is not read. A missing household is rejected.

## Changes

- `PlanningFactBasisRules` labels one input. A suggestion, an estimate, an
  imported amount, and any statement other than a saved bill are rejected if
  something tries to treat them as a new confirmed bill. The check does not
  save one.
- `IPlanningFacts` reads cash, pay, bills, suggestions, debts, living
  spending, and open-conversation notes through the services the screens
  use. Debt matches use the existing per-debt account list.

## Data changes

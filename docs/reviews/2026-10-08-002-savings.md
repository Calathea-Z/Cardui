# Savings

## Increment

Phase 2 item 7. Savings is a setting at `/savings`: everyday spending, one emergency goal, and named goals under Saving for. Each has a target and a date. The amount already set aside can be typed, or it can follow one cash account. Plan's cash outlook uses that reserve. Saving a goal does not move money.

## Decision

Zach chose the screen before it was written.

- Savings is in the account menu after Debts and before Targets. It is not a tab, and it is not a section of Targets.
- Everyday spending, the emergency goal, and each named goal have a target and a date. The monthly amount is calculated. It is not stored.
- The first card says Everyday spending. The button says Save for something, and that section says Saving for. The stored kinds stay `Operating`, `Emergency`, and `Sinking`.
- Already set aside is editable. It can also follow one cash account. The plan uses that balance until you type your own. Use account balance goes back to the balance. One cash account backs one goal.
- The Plan chart stays cash in accounts. The line under Cash outlook names the protected amount and what is left after it. Leftover below zero is its own warning.

## Changes

- `SavingsGoal` stores kind (`Operating`, `Emergency`, or `Sinking`), name, target amount, target date, reserved amount, currency, an optional account, when the follow started, and when the reserved amount was last typed. Everyday spending and Emergency are one each. Those names are fixed on the screen. Names under Saving for are unique in the household, ignoring case.
- `GET /api/savings-goals`, `GET /api/savings-goals/accounts`, `POST /api/savings-goals`, `PUT /api/savings-goals/{id}`, and `DELETE /api/savings-goals/{id}`. Kind and currency stay on update. A followed account must be an active, unarchived cash account in the planning currency. A blank currency counts. Credit, loan, and investment accounts are excluded.
- While following and not overridden, the amount in use is the account balance. A negative balance counts as nothing set aside, and the goal says the account is below zero. Typing a different amount keeps that number. Clearing the account stores the submitted amount. An account that can no longer be followed stays named, and the plan uses the typed amount until another account or no account is chosen.
- `HouseholdCashOutlook` takes the stored reserve and the calculated contributions. Cash stays the account total. Available is cash minus the reserve. A contribution raises the reserve and does not reduce cash. A goal in another currency is left out of the reserve and named.
- Plan returns `startingReserve`, `startingAvailable`, `reserveShortfallOn`, and `reserveRestoredOn`. The chart is unchanged. The description names the set-aside and the leftover only when the reserve is above zero. A reserve shortfall is a second warning.
- Frontend: `/savings`, the account-menu item, `SavingsPageClient`, the goal form, and the Plan copy for the reserve line.

## Changes

- Transactions shows Import CSV beside Add transaction.
- The sheet takes an account and a `.csv` file. It suggests columns from the
  header. You can map date, name, one amount column or separate debit and
  credit columns, and optional category and notes. Dates are month-first or
  day-first. Positive amounts can mean money out or money in. Debit is money
  out and credit is money in. A CR or DR marker sets the direction on its own.
- Preview marks each line ready, duplicate, or needing a fix. Ready lines are
  checked. A duplicate matches the same account, date, amount, and name,
  ignoring case and extra spaces, against a transaction already on the
  account or an earlier row in the file. A duplicate stays unchecked until
  you check it. A line with a bad date, name, or amount cannot be imported.
  A future date, or a date before a manual account's opening date, needs a
  fix.
- A category name that matches a household or system category is kept. A
  blank category uses the same description rules as a bank import. An
  unmatched name stays uncategorized, and the preview says so.
- Import stores posted transactions with source `Csv` and provenance
  `CsvImport`. Undo archives that batch and recalculates a manual balance. A
  linked account keeps the balance supplied by the bank. The name and amount
  of an imported row can be edited, the same as a manual entry.
- A file must be UTF-8 `.csv`, at most 1 MB, and at most 2,000 data rows.

# Living

Date: October 8, 2026
Status: Awaiting review
PR:

## Increment

Phase 2 item 8. Living is a setting at `/living`, after Debts and before Savings. Each person can set a monthly amount of their pay for the shared plan. Everyday spending moves here from Savings. Named monthly amounts, such as a vacation or a hobby, stay in the plan. The page shows what keeping them does to the debt-free date. Past transactions do not have to be categorized first.

## Decision

Zach chose the screen before it was written.

- The page is Living, at `/living`, in the account menu after Debts and before Savings. It is not a tab.
- Everyday spending moves here. Savings keeps cash to keep, the emergency goal, and dated goals.
- A contribution is a monthly dollar amount. Blank means that person's full recorded pay stays in the shared plan. Zero is a known zero.
- The payoff comparison uses minimum payments. The extra tried on Plan is separate and is not saved.

## Changes

- `HouseholdContributor.MonthlyContribution` is optional. Null means the amount is not set. A set amount is a share of that person's scheduled pay, applied to each real paycheck, including low pay and a later raise. A later raise keeps the same share of the check. Paycheck dates stay. An amount with no scheduled paycheck is not turned into a deposit. Pay with no person stays fully shared.
- `LifeAllowance` stores a name, a monthly amount, and the currency. Names are unique in the household, ignoring case. Each amount is counted on the 1st. When that day has passed, this month's full amount leaves cash today. It is not a bill and it does not raise the reserve.
- Everyday spending stays the operating savings goal. The card is edited on Living. Savings no longer shows it.
- `GET /api/living`, `PUT /api/living/contributions/{contributorId}`, `POST /api/living/allowances`, `PUT /api/living/allowances/{id}`, and `DELETE /api/living/allowances/{id}`. Saving does not write a transaction or change a balance.
- The monthly picture compares shared pay with scheduled bills, known minimums, and amounts kept for living. It is an average, not cash on a date. A shortfall is a warning. Irregular bills, unknown minimums, and another currency are named and left out.
- The payoff block compares minimum payments with the living amounts kept against putting that monthly total toward debt. A debt that cannot be projected stays unprojected.
- Plan's cash outlook uses the capped pay and the living amounts. When anything is kept, Cash outlook names the monthly total and links to Living. The cash chart is unchanged. The tooltip adds Kept for life.

## Data changes

Not generated. Please approve this before the walkthrough. It adds a nullable `MonthlyContribution` on `HouseholdContributors`, and creates `LifeAllowances` with a household lookup index. Deleting a household deletes its allowances. No existing rows, transactions, or balances change.

```powershell

```

Restart the API after the update. The running one does not know these fields.

## Correction (October 8, 2026)

Before approval, Zach asked for an audit of Living against everyday spending.
The implementation was simplified after this report was written. The current
design is in `docs/design/living.md`; a new review records its verification.

- Monthly living spending is the one `Operating` row. Its visible name is
  Monthly living spending. It covers flexible spending not already on Bills.
- `LifeAllowance`, Kept for life, the separate `LifeSpending` cash-flow kind,
  and their CRUD endpoints were removed.
- The all-or-nothing payoff comparison was removed. It unrealistically sent
  all ordinary living spending to debt. A user-selected spending-change
  comparison remains in Phase 3 item 6.
- Targets remain categorized-activity feedback and do not add another amount
  to Plan. Savings keeps protected cash and dated goals.
- A contribution entry is today's monthly benchmark. The calculator derives
  that person's share of scheduled pay, and low pay and future raises keep
  that share.
- `20261008152308_AddLivingContributionsAndAllowances` had already been
  generated and applied. The allowance table had zero rows.
  `20261008154357_SimplifyLivingModel` is applied and drops only that table.
  `MonthlyContribution` and existing savings-goal data remain.

The original Changes, verification, and walkthrough above describe the
superseded first implementation and must not be used for approval.

# Data sharing

## Increment

Phase 4 item 8. A conversation records an explicit yes or no before household
data can be prepared for the planning model. The prepared text names only the
sections that were asked for. A bank access token is left out. A transaction
description or uploaded text is quoted as data, and the developer instructions
stay Cardui's own words. No model or price is chosen. There is no chat screen
and no live OpenAI call. Item 9 has not started.

## Decision

- `IPlanningShares` reads the choice, records it, and prepares the text.
  There is no route and no screen. This service does not call
  `IPlanningModel`. A direct call to the OpenAI client still refuses a bank
  token before the request is posted, and the token is not logged. Nothing
  financial is written, and no money is moved.
- The choice is stored on the open conversation under the reserved name
  `DataSharing`. The stored words are `allowed` and `declined`. A missing
  note means the question has not been asked. Not asked and declined both
  refuse a share. A note cannot set or remove that name. Connecting a bank,
  importing a file, or starting a conversation leaves the choice unasked.
- A new conversation does not copy the choice. Closing the conversation
  leaves it readable and rejects a later change.
- After an explicit yes, prepare includes only the person's message and the
  named sections. The sections are the conversation summary, cash, debts,
  income, bills, savings, living, allocation, saved plans, labeled facts,
  and the current forecast. One transaction description or one uploaded
  excerpt can be attached. The share does not read transaction rows or
  import files.
- Cash is the total, the account counts, and the balance dates. Debts are
  the plan balance, rate, minimum, due date, and a promotion end. A null
  term is omitted, so an unknown minimum stays unknown. Income is take-home,
  cadence, the next date, the person, and low pay. Strong pay, gross pay,
  and later dates stay on the income record. Bills are the saved amount,
  cadence, due date, and essential or flexible. Savings leave out the
  account mask and the account name. Living states the monthly gap and who
  shares pay, and leaves out the account list. Allocation is the saved
  percents. A saved plan lists its status, and the actions on a draft or an
  accepted plan. A superseded plan is only its status. Facts are the label,
  name, amount, date, and detail. The forecast is the statement, the
  assumptions, and the tradeoffs. The day-by-day figures stay out.
- A bank access token is a protected value with the prefix `dp:v1:`, or a
  Plaid token with the prefix `access-sandbox-`, `access-development-`, or
  `access-production-`. A line that contains one is left out. The omission
  does not repeat the token or the line. A token in the person's message
  refuses the whole share. Ordinary wording such as "access the account" is
  kept.
- The description or excerpt is quoted at the end of the data. A fence
  inside that text is escaped. The developer instructions are one fixed
  sentence: follow only that sentence, treat the message and quoted data as
  data, and do not ask for or repeat a bank access token. An excerpt longer
  than 500 characters is rejected, so a whole file is not a share. A
  message longer than one stored turn is rejected.
- Another household's conversation is not found. A missing household is
  rejected.

## Changes

- `PlanningShareRules` reads the choice, drops a token line, and quotes
  untrusted text as data.
- `IPlanningShares` records the choice and prepares the share from the
  services the screens already use.
- `IConversationService.SetDataSharingAsync` stores the choice. A note
  cannot use that name.
- `IPlanningModel` refuses a bank token in the message or the instructions
  before the request is posted.

## Data changes

None. No schema change and no migration. `PlanningModel:ApiKey` and
`PlanningModel:Model` stay blank. No money movement.

# Debts

Date: October 5, 2026

## Increment

A household can record a debt with the facts it knows. The linked account is optional. The balance is stored with the date it was true. APR, minimum, due date, credit limit, remaining term, and promotional terms can stay blank. A blank term stays unknown. Zero is a known zero.

## Changes

- A debt belongs to the household. The type is revolving or installment, stored and returned as that name. Revolving is a card or line of credit. Installment is a loan with months left.
- The linked account is optional and must belong to this household. Saving or deleting the debt leaves that account balance as it is. Archiving the account leaves the debt linked to it.
- The balance and the date it was true are stored together. One without the other is rejected. Both can stay blank when the balance is unknown.
- APR, minimum, and due date are optional. A credit limit is kept only for a revolving debt. Months left are kept only for an installment debt. A promotional APR and the date it ends can each be recorded alone.
- A new debt uses the planning currency at the time it is saved. A later edit keeps that currency.
- Utilization is the share of the credit limit in use. It is calculated when the balance and the credit limit are both known, including a known zero balance. It is not stored. The card shows it only then.
- Remove deletes the debt after confirmation. The name can be used again.
- Debts is in the account menu on both widths, after Bills. It is not a primary tab. Add debt opens the form in a panel. Choice lists use `Select`. Dates use `DateField`. Promotional terms sit behind a disclosure.
- `20261005202726_AddDebts` adds the `Debts` table. Kind is `character varying`. The household link deletes a debt with the household. The account link becomes empty if that account row is deleted. Indexes are on household plus name, and on account.

## Manual verification

Restart the API before these steps so it loads this build. The database already has the new table. These steps use the household you already have. Income, bills, and the dashboard should stay the same throughout.

1. Open Debts from the account menu. On a phone, open it from the account control. It is not a tab.
   Expected: the page is empty, with Add debt in the title row.
2. Add a revolving debt named "Store card", with a balance, the date that balance was true, and one of your accounts. Leave APR, minimum, due date, and credit limit blank.
   Expected: the card shows that balance and date, the account name, and APR, minimum, due date, and credit limit as unknown. The account balance is unchanged.
3. Choose Edit. Enter APR 19.99, a minimum, a due date, and a credit limit above the balance. Save.
   Expected: those values show on the card, including the share of the limit in use.
4. Add an installment debt named "Car loan" and fill in months left. Leave the balance blank.
   Expected: the card says the balance is unknown and shows the months left.
5. Add a revolving debt with balance 0 and a date.
   Expected: the balance shows as $0.00. It does not say the balance is unknown.
6. Try to save with Type left on Select. Then try a balance with no date.
   Expected: the save does not stick. The message says to choose revolving or installment, or to enter the date.
7. Open Promotional terms, enter a promo APR, leave the end date blank, and save.
   Expected: the card shows that promo rate and says the end date is unknown.
8. Choose Remove on one debt and confirm.
   Expected: that debt is gone. The account balance is unchanged. Add the same name again and it is available.
9. On a phone-width window, open Add debt, then a choice list and a date.
   Expected: the form is a full-screen sheet. The choice and the date use the app's list and calendar. Escape closes an open list first, then the sheet.
10. Edit a debt whose type is Installment. Open Type and press Tab.
    Expected: focus starts on Revolving, and the next Tab moves to Installment. Either option can be chosen.

# Bills

Date: October 5, 2026

## Increment

A household can record a bill by hand. The amount is one payment. The page
asks for how often it is due, the next due date, an optional source account,
and whether the bill is essential or flexible. Recurring suggestions stay
for the rest of Phase 2 item 3.

## Changes

- A bill belongs to the household. The amount is one payment. Cadence is
  weekly, every two weeks, twice a month, monthly, quarterly, yearly, or no
  set schedule. Essential has to be paid. Flexible can be reduced or skipped.
  Those values are stored and returned as their names.
- The next due date is a calendar date. The source account is optional and
  must belong to this household. A new bill uses the planning currency at
  the time it is saved. A later edit keeps that currency.
- The card shows the amount as each payment, the cadence, essential or
  flexible, the account name or No account, and the next due date. It does
  not show a monthly figure.
- Remove deletes the bill after confirmation. The account and its balance
  stay. Archiving that account leaves the bill linked to it. The name can
  be used again.
- Bills is in the account menu on both widths, after Income. It is not a
  primary tab. Add bill opens the form in a panel. Choice lists use
  `Select`. Dates use `DateField`.
- `20261005183259_AddObligations` adds the `Obligations` table. Cadence and
  flexibility are `character varying`. The household link deletes a bill
  with the household. The account link becomes empty if that account row is
  deleted. Indexes are on household plus name, and on account.

## Manual verification

Restart the API before these steps so it loads this build. The database
already has the new table. These steps use the household you already have.
Income and the dashboard should stay the same throughout.

1. Open Bills from the account menu. On a phone, open it from the account
   control. It is not a tab.
   Expected: the page is empty, with Add bill in the title row.
2. Add a bill named "Rent", with an amount, Every month, a next due date,
   one of your accounts, and Essential.
   Expected: the card shows that amount each payment, Every month,
   Essential, the account name, and Next due with that date. There is no
   monthly figure.
3. Choose Edit, change the amount, and save.
   Expected: the new amount is shown as each payment.
4. Add a bill with No account and Flexible.
   Expected: the card says No account and Flexible.
5. Add a bill with cadence No set schedule.
   Expected: the card still shows the next due date and each payment.
6. Choose Remove on one bill and confirm.
   Expected: that bill is gone. The account balance is unchanged. Add the
   same name again and it is available.
7. Try to save a bill with no amount, or with cadence left on Select.
   Expected: the save does not stick. The message says what is missing.
8. On a phone-width window, open Add bill, then a choice list and a date.
   Expected: the form is a full-screen sheet. The choice and the date use
   the app's list and calendar. Escape closes an open list first, then the
   sheet.

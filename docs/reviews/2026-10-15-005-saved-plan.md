# Saved plan

## Increment

Phase 3 items 10 and 11. A household can keep one draft and one accepted plan, with older copies superseded. Your plan states the next actions in plain language and can be printed from the browser.

## What you see first

On Plan, **Your plan** is the fifth tab. The answer is the ordered actions, at most three, and whether this version is the one to keep. The leading button is **Save draft**, **Accept plan** when that draft matches the preview, or **Print** when the accepted plan matches. Assumptions, risks, missing information, milestones, and review conditions stay closed until opened. Older versions stay in a history disclosure. On a phone the same content stacks, and the tab bar stays two columns. Print hides the shell, the tabs, and the scenario controls, and it opens the supporting sections.

## Decision

- A preview is not stored. **Save draft** writes an immutable snapshot: facts, assumptions, priorities, the selected scenario, actions, milestones, and review conditions. Later edits to debts, pay, or bills do not change that row.
- One household has at most one draft and one accepted plan. Saving a different plan replaces the open draft. Accepting a draft marks the previous accepted plan superseded. Accepting the plan that is already accepted returns it. A stale draft or accept is a conflict.
- The selected scenario is roll payments forward or free up cash. A partial reclaim is not a saved plan.
- The three actions are ranked from the current recovery report. A cash shortfall leads. A debt with no balance asks for terms before it asks for a payment. A rolled payment names the next debt. Freeing up cash does not.
- Sentences are built in the browser from those fields. The stored document does not contain display sentences.
- Print uses the browser. There is no PDF library and no public link. Cardui does not pay a bill, pay a debt, or move money.

## Changes

- Domain: `DecisionPlanRules` ranks the actions and the supporting facts. `PlanVersionRules` decides create, replace, unchanged, accept, already accepted, or stale.
- `PlanVersions` stores the snapshot as JSON, with draft and accepted uniqueness per household.
- Plan: the Your plan tab, the preview, save, accept, history, and browser print.

## Data changes

`20261016030137_AddPlanVersions` is applied. It adds `PlanVersions` only. Household delete cascades. `(HouseholdId, Number)` is unique. Status is stored as its member name. The document is `jsonb`. No existing table changes. No money movement.

## Manual verification

Restart the API if it was running before `AddPlanVersions`.

1. Open Plan, then Your plan, before saving.
   Expected: The status is Not saved. The actions match the preview. Save draft is the leading button. The page says Cardui does not move money.
2. Save the draft, change a scenario, then save again. Accept the draft that matches the preview.
   Expected: The first save is a draft. A different preview replaces that draft. Accept marks it accepted and leaves the older copy superseded. Saving the same plan again does not add a second draft.
3. Print the accepted plan.
   Expected: The browser print shows the actions and the supporting sections. The sidebar, tabs, and scenario controls are not on the page.
4. Change a debt or a bill after accepting.
   Expected: The accepted version stays as it was. The live preview can differ until you save a new draft.
